## Change scope
1. **The provider layer applies `email_verified`.** `IdClaims` reads `email_verified` (absent or non-boolean = not verified); `finish_login` puts `Some(email)` into `VerifiedIdentity.email` **only when `email_verified` is exactly `true`**, else `None`. Document the rule in the type's doc comment (one sentence: "the email is a hint the provider vouches for; an unverified address is dropped here, not by the caller"). Tests, with the TLS stand-in of `oidc_private_provider.rs`: an ID token with `email` and `email_verified: true` → `Some`; `false` → `None`; **absent** → `None`; `"true"` as a string → `None`; no `email` at all → `None`; and `provider_held.rs`'s account-row-unchanged assertions still hold. **Mutation:** the check removed → the `false` and the string case fail. Nothing else about sign-in changes (the authenticator still ignores the email; no storage).
2. **`docs/accounts.md` says what was measured.** In "What the provider told us, and what planeter does about it" and wherever the page says the test has not run: replace "has not run / open" by **the measured result**, in plain words, **limited exactly as ID-11 limits it**: the tag `0.92.0`, in process against a local build behind a throw-away certificate authority, the nine kinds of check and what each showed (a sentence or two each: do not copy the report), **the sentence "planeter's sign-in code was tested with sui-id 0.92.0 on 2026-11-27"** (the ledger's wording, not stronger) **with its limits in the next sentence** (not through a running planeter server; a private certificate authority is not supported yet; what was not shown). ID-1…ID-7 are now *measured*, ID-8 and ID-9 remain *told* (they are not things a test shows), ID-10 is *done*, ID-11 and ID-12 are cited. Say plainly the three differences of ID-12 only as far as an **operator of planeter** needs them (the first-run commands of sui-id's `main` documents are not those of the `0.92.0` binary; the console box; the login rate limit behind a proxy), under one short "If you use sui-id" heading, and **name no release other than `0.92.0`** and no claim about `main`. `crates/planeter-web/tests/accounts_doc.rs` follows: it must hold the sentence's exact words, the version, the date, that the limits are on the page, that ID-11 and ID-12 exist in the ledger, and **still fail on a "tested with sui-id" claim that has another version, no date or no limit beside it** (extend its mutation for that).
3. **Leave alone:** the ledger (the architect's), the letter, `docs/RELEASING.md`, the interoperability test file, the threat model, SCHEDULE and STATUS.

## Non-change scope
No other code; no schema; no catalog text; no sign-off record, baseline, `ja.signed` or `SignOff`; no new dependency or `unsafe`; no tag, release or upload.

## Acceptance criteria
The provider layer returns an email only when it is verified, shown by the five cases and a mutation; `docs/accounts.md` states the measured result with the exact sentence and its limits and nothing stronger, held by the document test; `tools/gates.sh --tier review` green on the commit (with a sheet generated for it) and CI green on all eight jobs.

## Required evidence
Commits (hash → what, each compiling and passing the tests it touches); the five test cases' names; the mutation; the page's new section and the document test's additions with their mutation; the review-tier table the script printed; deviations and open questions.
