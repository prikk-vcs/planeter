## Non-change scope
No tag; no GitHub release; no crates.io upload; no ghcr change; no feature or fix beyond the files named; no dependency change (T5 aside, by table); no change to a default; no catalog text change; **no Japanese sign-off or baseline change**; no rehearsal unless T5 says the chain changed.

## Acceptance criteria
T1–T7 with their evidence; the candidate commit on `main`, everything green; the CHANGELOG section readable as release notes without editing facts; the count of unread Japanese messages stated in the notes and equal to the tool's.

## Prohibited shortcuts
No tag, no upload; no "publish" step run even as a test; no edit of a historical CHANGELOG entry; no weakening of a gate; no `ignore`; no sign-off record written for text the owner has not read.

## Required evidence and review-request format
Commits (hash → what, by task); T1's status line, sheet header and fingerprint and the start-up line; the `cargo package --list` summaries and the dry-run result; the pins table and the chain diff; T7's saved pages and JSON and the two start-up lines; every gate with command, exit code, tool versions (prikk, cargo, cargo-deny, cargo-audit) and the advisory-database HEAD; deviations and open questions.
