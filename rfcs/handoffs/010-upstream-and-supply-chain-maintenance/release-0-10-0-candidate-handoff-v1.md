## Change scope
- **T1 — version and CHANGELOG.** `[workspace.package] version = "0.10.0"` and every `path` dependency of the eleven published crates and `tools/i18n-sheet` (the lock follows; `--locked` works). The CHANGELOG section is written as release notes a reader who knows nothing of our work can act on: **what is new for a person who runs a server** (the account and token commands; what a person sees in their own log; the sign-in settings and their defaults), **what an upgrader must do or expect** (a table or a setting that is new; what happens to existing accounts; that no repository is touched; the database changes, said exactly; the first start's behaviour), **what was fixed** (IS-74 and IS-75 in the words of a user: *a read, an export or a changes page could be answered with an error while prikk was writing; it is not any more*; say they were in 0.9.0, that they were availability and not security, and that they failed in the safe direction), **what was measured and what was not** (the sui-id sentence **exactly** as `docs/accounts.md` has it, with its limits, and nothing stronger), **what is not built** (self-service pages, provisioning, collaborators, "key via delivery", a private certificate authority for the provider, a refresh check of the person at the provider). Every fact has a source (a test, a command output, a SCHEDULE entry); list them in the report. **The gates tooling is in the notes in one short paragraph, as the maintainers' concern, not the operator's.** No claim of "secure", "safe" or "compatible with" beyond a measured sentence.
- **T2 — the Japanese not yet signed off.** `planeter-i18n-sheet --lang ja --status` gives the exact line (**160 messages expected** at `796160e`, plus any the later fixes added: say the number the tool gives and why it differs, if it does; both the server's and the client's counts). They **ship flagged** exactly as 0.9.0's did unless the owner has signed some off by then (the owner may read some groups first; if a sign-off record exists when you start, say so and follow it, do not make one). The reading-order file (`accounts-0-10-0-reading-order.md`) is regenerated to the tool's counts: group counts must sum to the status line. **No catalog text is changed, no Japanese is signed off, no baseline is edited by you.**
- **T3 — the crates.** Eleven published crates in the RELEASING order (none new); `cargo package --list -p <crate> --locked` for each, the grep for private material; `cargo publish --workspace --exclude planeter-runner --dry-run --locked` in a clean worktree as for 0.9.0.
- **T4 — the documents.** `README.md` (status paragraph for 0.10.0; the capability line for accounts; the "Not yet" list as in T1), `ROADMAP.md` (the 0.10.0 row is the candidate until tagged), `docs/STATUS.md` and `docs/SCHEDULE.md` are **mine**: do not edit them; list in the report any line you think is stale. `docs/RELEASING.md`: the log row for 0.10.0 (as 0.9.0's), and the tiers paragraph read once against the real script (`tools/gates.sh --help`) so the two say the same.
- **T5 — the pins review (RELEASING step 5).** The table for every `uses:` SHA, the base-image digest (new index since the 0.9.0 one? compare packages as in 0.9.0), the BuildKit pin, the compiler pin (1.99.1: still the newest we use? say what is newer and why we stay), the prikk pin (0.58.0 still the floor: **is there a newer prikk release? If yes, say so and do not move the pin**; that is a decision of mine with its own handoff), every third-party dependency's age and advisories. A pin that moves is its own commit with its own record, and only with my approval before the candidate is built.
- **T6 — the gates, immediately before handing over,** on the final candidate commit, in a clean worktree: fresh advisory database, `cargo deny check` and `cargo audit` 0 with no ignore, fmt, floor-check, pin-check, clippy, **the release tier of `tools/gates.sh`** (it needs `--sheet FILE`: the Japanese reading sheet the owner reads; **generate it from the candidate's catalogs with `planeter-i18n-sheet --lang ja --out`** and hand the file's path and fingerprint in the report; the sheet that the owner reads is the one of the candidate), with the real prikk. Include the table the script prints, whole. **Run it with the capped runner as it is by default**; do not raise a limit.
- **T7 — an end-to-end look at the candidate**, as 0.9.0's T7, extended for accounts. Build the **release** binaries (`PLANETER_GIT_COMMIT` set) and run them for real against real prikk 0.58.0 with a spool and an artifact directory, saving pages, JSON and transcripts. **In addition:** (a) with the server **running**, create an account, list it, create a token (shown once on one stdout line, and to a 0600 file), list tokens, revoke one, disable the account, and show **each of the six principal paths refusing at the next request**; save the transcripts and the account's own log page; (b) the same commands with a server **stopped**; (c) start with `PLANETER_LOCAL_PASSWORDS=off` and show a password sign-in refused and an account's hash kept; (d) a sign-in through the TLS stand-in provider of `crates/planeter-auth/tests/oidc_private_provider.rs`'s kind, **through the running server** (the thing the sui-id sentence says was *not* done: do it, report what it showed; **do not change the sentence on the page**: if the result would allow a stronger one, say so and I decide); (e) kill the operator's account command at each failpoint and show it finished at the next start, as for keys; (f) the 1,000-repository figures and the kill-and-restart log as in 0.9.0.

## Non-change scope
No tag; no GitHub release; no crates.io upload; no ghcr change; no feature or fix beyond the files named; no dependency change (T5 by table); no change to a default; **no catalog text change; no Japanese sign-off; no baseline edit**; no change to the operator's commands or to any threshold; no edit of `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/SCHEDULE.md`, the ledgers, the threat model or any security review (mine: I write T-22, RR-22 and `docs/security-review-0.10.0.md` against the candidate).

## Acceptance criteria
T1–T8 with their evidence; the candidate commit on `main`, everything green (CI on that commit, and the release tier from your own run); the CHANGELOG readable as release notes without editing facts; the counts of unread Japanese stated in the notes and equal to the tool's; the reading-order file's counts equal to the tool's; every claim of T7(d) bounded by what was run.

## Prohibited shortcuts
No tag, no upload; no "publish" step run even as a test; no edit of a historical CHANGELOG entry; no weakening of a gate; no `ignore`; no sign-off record for text the owner has not read; no pin move without its record; no stronger sentence about sui-id than the page's.

## Required evidence and review-request format
Commits (hash → what, by task); T1's CHANGELOG with every fact's source; T2's status line, sheet header and fingerprint, the reading-order counts; the `cargo package --list` summaries and the dry-run result; the pins table; T7's saved pages, JSON, transcripts, the figures and the kill-and-restart log; the gates with commands, exit codes, tool versions, the advisory-database HEAD and count; deviations and open questions (say plainly what you could not show). Final reply: the review-request path only.
