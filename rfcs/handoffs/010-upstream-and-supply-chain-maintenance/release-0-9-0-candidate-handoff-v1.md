## Non-change scope
No tag; no GitHub release; no crates.io upload; no ghcr change; no feature or fix beyond the files named; no dependency change (T5 by table); no change to a default; **no catalog text change; no Japanese sign-off; no baseline edit**; no change to the operator command, the delivery engine, the API or the client other than the version.

## Acceptance criteria
T1–T8 with their evidence; the candidate commit on `main`, everything green; the CHANGELOG readable as release notes without editing facts; the counts of unread Japanese stated in the notes and equal to the tool's; the reading-order file's counts equal to the tool's; the upgrade from a real 0.8.0 data directory shown.

## Prohibited shortcuts
No tag, no upload; no "publish" step run even as a test; no edit of a historical CHANGELOG entry; no weakening of a gate; no `ignore`; no sign-off record for text the owner has not read; no pin move without its record.

## Required evidence and review-request format
Commits (hash → what, by task); T1's CHANGELOG diff and every fact's source; T2's status line, sheet header and fingerprint, the reading-order counts; the `cargo package --list` summaries and the dry-run result; the pins table; T7's saved pages, JSON, transcripts, the 1,000-repository figures and the kill-and-restart log; the gates with commands, exit codes, tool versions, the advisory-database HEAD and count; deviations and open questions (say plainly what you could not show).
